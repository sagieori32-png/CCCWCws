About the Center

Engaging in Critical Childhood Studies — a German–Israeli collaboration from Frankfurt and Jerusalem.

Childhood research at both universities stands at the forefront of international scholarship. It is closely connected to pioneering work in the social sciences on childhood and the family. At the core of the planned CCCWC are studies on children's well-being and rights, with a particular interest in child welfare, vulnerability, violence, abuse, and neglect.

Since 2015, international data sets on the subjective well-being of children aged eight to twelve have been available through ISCIWeB. This study, founded and led by the research group leaders at both universities, was made possible by a 1.5 million euro grant from the Jacobs Foundation in Zurich. The data reveal both commonalities and significant differences in the experiences of childhood in Israel and Germany.

Since 2009, the leaders of the research teams have worked together intensively and have earned international recognition in childhood studies. Their collaboration has focused especially on child well-being, children's participation in research, and vulnerability — with a particular emphasis on child sexual abuse.

Objectives

Three pillars: research, qualification, and teaching.

The planned center will develop three key areas that form the foundation of its work in critical childhood studies. Each will be expanded to contribute fresh and innovative perspectives to the international academic community.

◆

Research Agenda

  • Identifying and developing selected research foci, such as Digital Childhoods and Participation, and Emotional Violence from the Perspective of Children
  • Expanding networks among childhood researchers in Frankfurt and Jerusalem
  • Advancing the field of critical German–Israeli childhood research
  • Strengthening connections with migration research
  • Preparing research proposals for funding bodies including GIF, DFG, Minerva, and other foundations
  • Contributing to global studies such as the Children's Worlds Study
★

Early-Career Development

  • Supporting joint publications in international journals by PhD students and postdoctoral researchers from Frankfurt and Jerusalem
  • Organizing annual workshops on innovative topics and methodological challenges in critical childhood research
  • Facilitating research stays for early-career researchers at partner institutions
▲

Teaching & Curriculum

  • Developing courses and fostering Master's-level student networks focused on child maltreatment, well-being, migration, displacement, poverty, and vulnerability
  • Organizing annual compact events and building on established seminar formats
  • Designing and evaluating hybrid teaching approaches
  • Promoting internationalization in Educational Science (Frankfurt) and Social Work (Jerusalem)
